# Sustainable Development

A project exploring how software can advance the United Nations' **17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**.

This site collects ideas, notes, and resources for building tech-enabled solutions aligned with the SDGs — from clean water sensors to climate risk dashboards.

## What's here

- **[SDG Project Ideas](sdg-ideas.md)** — concrete, buildable project ideas mapped to each of the 17 goals.

## About the SDGs

The [17 Sustainable Development Goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals) were adopted by all UN Member States in 2015 as a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity for people and the planet, now and into the future. They cover everything from ending poverty and hunger to climate action and strong institutions.
