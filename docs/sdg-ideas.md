# Sustainable Development Goals — Project Ideas

This document lists concrete project/product ideas mapped to each of the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Each idea is scoped to be buildable as a software/tech-enabled solution (web app, mobile app, data platform, IoT system, etc.) and can serve as a starting point for this repository.

---

## 1. No Poverty
- **Micro-grant matching platform**: Connects low-income individuals/small entrepreneurs with local micro-grants, subsidies, and interest-free micro-loans based on eligibility.
- **Financial literacy & budgeting app**: Simple, offline-friendly app teaching budgeting, saving, and debt management in local languages.
- **Digital ID + benefits tracker**: Helps citizens discover and apply for government welfare schemes they qualify for but may not know about.
- **Gig-work income smoothing tool**: Predicts and smooths irregular income for informal workers using simple savings automation.

## 2. Zero Hunger
- **Farm-to-surplus marketplace**: Connects farmers with surplus produce to food banks, restaurants, and consumers at discounted rates to reduce waste.
- **Crop yield & soil health advisor**: Mobile app using weather + soil data to recommend planting schedules and fertilizer use for smallholder farmers.
- **Community food bank locator**: Real-time map of food banks, soup kitchens, and their current stock/needs.
- **School meal program tracker**: Dashboard for NGOs/governments to monitor nutrition and coverage of school feeding programs.

## 3. Good Health and Well-Being
- **Telemedicine for rural areas**: Low-bandwidth video/chat consultation platform connecting rural patients with doctors.
- **Vaccination reminder & tracking system**: SMS/app-based reminders for immunization schedules, especially for children in low-resource settings.
- **Mental health check-in chatbot**: Anonymous, stigma-free chatbot offering coping resources and escalation to professionals.
- **Disease outbreak early-warning dashboard**: Aggregates community-reported symptoms to flag potential outbreaks before they spread.

## 4. Quality Education
- **Offline-first e-learning app**: Downloadable lessons/videos for areas with poor internet connectivity, syncing progress when online.
- **Peer-to-peer tutoring platform**: Matches students needing help with volunteer tutors/mentors by subject and language.
- **Teacher resource-sharing hub**: Open repository of lesson plans, worksheets, and assessments curated by grade/subject.
- **Adaptive literacy app for children**: Gamified reading app that adjusts difficulty based on a child's progress.

## 5. Gender Equality
- **Safe reporting platform for harassment/violence**: Anonymous, secure reporting and resource-referral tool for survivors.
- **Women-in-workforce mentorship network**: Connects women with mentors in male-dominated industries.
- **Pay equity analyzer for organizations**: Tool for HR teams to audit and visualize gender pay gaps.
- **Girls' STEM education tracker**: Dashboard tracking enrollment/retention of girls in STEM programs to identify dropout risk.

## 6. Clean Water and Sanitation
- **Water quality crowdsourcing app**: Citizens report water quality issues (color, smell, test-strip readings) with GPS tagging, aggregated on a public map.
- **Smart handpump/well monitoring (IoT)**: Sensors detect pump failures in rural wells and alert maintenance teams instantly.
- **Household water usage tracker**: Helps families monitor and reduce water consumption with tips and leak alerts.
- **Sanitation facility mapping tool**: Crowdsourced map of public toilets/sanitation facilities with cleanliness ratings.

## 7. Affordable and Clean Energy
- **Solar panel ROI calculator**: Estimates cost savings and payback period for household/business solar installation by region.
- **Community microgrid management dashboard**: Monitors and balances load for off-grid solar/wind community microgrids.
- **Energy access mapping platform**: Visualizes electrification gaps to prioritize infrastructure investment.
- **Peer-to-peer renewable energy trading app**: Lets households with solar panels sell excess energy to neighbors.

## 8. Decent Work and Economic Growth
- **Fair-wage job board**: Job listings verified for fair pay and safe working conditions, targeting informal/gig sectors.
- **Skills-to-jobs matching platform**: Matches unemployed individuals' skills to local job openings or reskilling programs.
- **Small business formalization toolkit**: Guides informal businesses through registration, tax compliance, and access to credit.
- **Labor rights education chatbot**: Explains worker rights and how to report violations in simple language.

## 9. Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- **Infrastructure project transparency dashboard**: Tracks public infrastructure spending, timelines, and completion status.
- **Local innovation/startup incubator platform**: Connects grassroots innovators with mentors, funding, and prototyping resources.
- **Rural broadband coverage mapper**: Crowdsources internet speed/availability data to guide infrastructure investment.
- **Predictive maintenance for public infrastructure (IoT)**: Sensors on bridges/roads flag structural issues before failure.

## 10. Reduced Inequalities
- **Accessibility audit tool for public services/websites**: Scans and scores digital services for accessibility compliance (WCAG).
- **Inclusive hiring platform**: Anonymizes resumes to reduce bias and tracks diversity metrics for employers.
- **Migrant/refugee resource navigator**: Multilingual app listing legal aid, housing, healthcare, and job resources.
- **Disability-friendly transit planner**: Routes public transit trips accounting for wheelchair access and other needs.

## 11. Sustainable Cities and Communities
- **Participatory city budgeting platform**: Lets residents propose and vote on local infrastructure/community projects.
- **Air quality monitoring network (IoT + app)**: Low-cost sensors feeding a public real-time air quality map with health alerts.
- **Smart waste collection routing**: Optimizes garbage truck routes using fill-level sensors in bins to cut emissions/cost.
- **Affordable housing finder**: Aggregates subsidized/affordable housing listings with eligibility screening.

## 12. Responsible Consumption and Production
- **Food waste tracking app for households/restaurants**: Logs waste, suggests recipes for leftovers, and tracks savings over time.
- **Product sustainability scorecard**: Browser extension rating products by carbon footprint, packaging, and supply chain ethics.
- **Circular economy marketplace**: Platform for buying/selling/repairing used goods to extend product lifecycles.
- **Supply chain transparency tracker**: Blockchain or database-backed traceability for ethical sourcing claims.

## 13. Climate Action
- **Personal/household carbon footprint calculator**: Tracks emissions from travel, energy, and diet with reduction tips.
- **Climate risk mapping for communities**: Visualizes flood/heat/wildfire risk by neighborhood using open climate data.
- **Reforestation tracking platform**: Maps tree-planting projects and monitors survival rates via volunteer/satellite data.
- **Extreme weather early-warning app**: Push notifications for heatwaves, floods, and storms tailored to user location.

## 14. Life Below Water
- **Ocean plastic pollution reporting app**: Crowdsourced beach/ocean litter reporting with photo geotagging for cleanup coordination.
- **Sustainable seafood guide app**: Scans/searches seafood products to show sustainability ratings and alternatives.
- **Illegal fishing detection dashboard**: Aggregates vessel tracking (AIS) data to flag suspicious fishing activity in protected zones.
- **Coral reef health monitoring platform**: Citizen-science photo uploads analyzed (with ML) to track bleaching over time.

## 15. Life on Land
- **Deforestation alert system**: Uses satellite imagery (e.g., open data feeds) to notify authorities/NGOs of illegal logging in near real-time.
- **Wildlife poaching reporting network**: Secure, anonymous tip line and ranger-coordination app for protected areas.
- **Urban biodiversity tracker**: Citizen-science app for logging local flora/fauna sightings to build biodiversity maps.
- **Reforestation crowdfunding platform**: Lets donors fund and track specific tree-planting projects with progress updates.

## 16. Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions
- **Corruption/bribery anonymous reporting app**: Secure whistleblower tool with case tracking for civil society/journalists.
- **Legal aid access platform**: Connects low-income individuals with free/low-cost legal assistance and self-help legal guides.
- **Government transparency dashboard**: Visualizes public budgets, spending, and procurement contracts for accountability.
- **Civic engagement/voting information app**: Provides nonpartisan election info, polling locations, and ballot measure explainers.

## 17. Partnerships for the Goals
- **NGO/nonprofit collaboration hub**: Directory and matchmaking platform for NGOs, donors, and governments to co-fund projects and avoid duplication.
- **Open SDG data aggregator**: Central dashboard pulling public SDG indicator data (UN, World Bank, etc.) for a country/region.
- **Volunteer & skills-matching platform**: Connects skilled volunteers (developers, designers, doctors) with NGO projects needing help.
- **Cross-sector funding tracker**: Tracks public/private/philanthropic funding flows toward specific SDG targets to identify gaps.
